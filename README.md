# AMERICAN SALES ANAYLYSIS
**Project Overview**
**Objective**
This project is a USA Retail Sales Data Analysis and Business Intelligence Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, products, stores, regions, and sales teams.

**Scope**
The project uses MySQL for data management and SQL analysis and Power BI for data visualization and dashboard development. The data was cleaned, organized, and combined into a Power BI-ready dataset to make it easier to analyze key business metrics.

The analysis focuses on important KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity Sold, Average Order Value, and Profit Margin. It also examines sales trends across different months, regions, sales channels, brands, products, stores, customers, warehouses, and sales teams.
**Methodology**
The final Power BI report consists of five interactive dashboards:

1. Executive Overview – provides a high-level summary of the company’s overall performance.
2. Sales & Orders – analyzes sales trends, orders, sales channels, and regional performance.
3. Products & Categories – examines product and category revenue and profitability.
4. Regions, Stores & Customer – analyzes regional, store, and customer performance.
5. Sales Team Performance & Operations – evaluates sales team performance and operational metrics.

The main objective of the project is to transform raw retail sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.
